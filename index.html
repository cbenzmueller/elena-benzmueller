Introduction

About me

My name is Elena and I live in Berlin. I love sports, particularly field hockey and gymnastics. I started gymnastics at TuS Lichterfelde in 2017/18 and field hockey around 2019, when I joined the Zehlendorfer Wespen. I was a member of the Berlin state hockey selection squad (Landeskader) in 2023 and again in 2026.

From 2023 to 2025 I lived in Dubai. During that time I focused primarily on gymnastics and joined Aspire Gymnastics Dubai, where I won the Level 4 and Level 5 all-around titles of the club competitions and took the all-around silver at Level 6. I also regularly entered running competitions for Ultimate Athletics Dubai and continued to play some hockey.

After returning to Germany in autumn 2025 I put hockey first again — and in October 2026 we won the German championship with the Wespen's girls' U16, after the Berlin title, an intermediate round and a penalty-shootout semi-final at the Youth Finals in Mönchengladbach. In the same year I played for the Berlin selection at the DHB Herbstpokal and was nominated for a U16 national team scouting training. At the same time I ran new bests over 5 km and 10 km, which brought me three top-50 listings in the German athletics rankings. Also take a look at the sites of my twin sister Carla and my brother Max, who are both into sports.

Zehlendorfer Wespen wU16 — German champions 2026

Berlin · field season 2026 · defence · shirt number 75

The road to the title ran through three rounds. We finished the Berlin main round third and won the Berlin final round (6:0 in the final against Berliner HC); as the East's No. 1 seed we won intermediate round C against Rot-Weiss Köln and MTV Braunschweig; and at the Youth Finals in Mönchengladbach we beat HTC Uhlenhorst Mülheim in a penalty shootout and Münchner SC in the final. It was the Wespen's first German field title in this age group — the girls' U16 had won the indoor championship in 2021/22 — and the first girls' U16 field final for both clubs in this millennium — and my twin sister Carla was in the team with me.
